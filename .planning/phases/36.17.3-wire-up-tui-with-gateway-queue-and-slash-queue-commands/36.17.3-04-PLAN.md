<objective>
Add the production-side queue infrastructure to the TUI: ownership fields on
`App`, the drain method with the in-flight guard, the post-turn drain hook,
and the status-bar pill. This plan does NOT touch the slash-command surface
(Plan 05's job) — it lays the receptacle.

Purpose: D-03 (App owns queue + fixed key), D-04 + D-05 (auto-drain hybrid +
sequential), D-09 status-bar half. T-04 (deadlock between drain and submit)
is mitigated by the in-flight guard in `maybe_drain_queue`.

Output: Compiles, status bar renders the pill, drain hook fires at the right
arm in `handle_stream_event` but does the right thing only when conditions
hold (no live queue items → no-op; queue paused → no-op; turn already in
flight → no-op).
</objective>
