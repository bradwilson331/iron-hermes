## Production DecomposeFn Wiring (RESEARCH §Q1 Open Question)

Per RESEARCH §Q1 recommendation (b), the gateway runner does NOT wire a production DecomposeFn in v1. The dispatcher ships with `decompose_fn: None` initialized by both constructors. Operators who set `kanban.auto_decompose: true` get a graceful no-op tick (Step 0 double-gate: outer gate passes, inner `if let Some(...)` short-circuits — zero LLM call, zero work, zero noise).

Future phase: wire a production DecomposeFn closure into `DispatcherContext` in the gateway runner. No dispatcher changes needed — the Step 0 architecture is in place.
