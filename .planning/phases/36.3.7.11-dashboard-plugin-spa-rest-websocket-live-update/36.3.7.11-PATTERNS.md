## Cross-Cutting Reminders (Dioxus 0.7.1 Rules)

1. **No `cx`, `Scope`, or `use_state`** — these are Dioxus 0.6 APIs removed in 0.7. Use `use_signal`, `use_memo`, `use_resource`, `use_context_provider` / `use_context`. Source: `crates/iron_hermes_ui/CLAUDE.md`.

2. **No signal borrow across `.await`** — `clippy.toml` enforces this. `WriteLock`, `GenerationalRef`, `GenerationalRefMut` held across an `.await` point causes runtime panic. Always drop the write guard before `spawn(async move { ... })`. Source: `clippy.toml` + `crates/iron_hermes_ui/CLAUDE.md`.

3. **`event.prevent_default()` lives on the outer `Event` wrapper, not `DragData`** — In Dioxus 0.7.1, `ondragover` receives `Event<DragData>`; `prevent_default()` is a method on the outer `Event<T>` type. Without it, `ondrop` silently never fires. Source: RESEARCH Risk 4.

4. **`DragEvent` and `DataTransfer` must be explicit in `Cargo.toml` `web-sys` features** — currently absent from `Cargo.toml` lines 24–38; they are pulled transitively. A clean build or dep bump can silently drop them. Add before any drag-and-drop code compiles. Source: RESEARCH Risk 3.

5. **`WheelWedge` modulo-10 → modulo-11 audit** — Adding `Kanban` (index 10) requires updating `from_index` to `rem_euclid(11)` AND adding match arm `10 => WheelWedge::Kanban`. ALL six methods (`index`, `from_index`, `label`, `sub`, `glyph`, `to_screen`) must be updated in a single commit. Failure causes index 10 to silently route to Chat. Source: `state.rs` lines 653–654, RESEARCH Risk 1.

6. **All hooks register unconditionally before conditional RSX** — `use_resource` (not `use_server_future(...)?`) avoids early-return hook ordering bugs. See agents.rs UAT-2 hotfix comment (lines 34–58). Source: `screens/agents.rs`.

7. **`KanbanStore` is `!Send`** — Wrap in `Arc<TokioMutex<KanbanStore>>`. Lock scope MUST end before any `.await` call that crosses the lock boundary. Do not hold the `MutexGuard` across `broadcast::Sender::send`. Source: RESEARCH Risk 2, `notifier.rs` pattern.

8. **`broadcast::Receiver` drop = implicit unsubscribe** — No explicit unsubscribe call needed for WS clients. When `kanban_ws.rs` WS handler exits, the `Receiver` is dropped, automatically removing the client from the broadcast fan-out. Source: RESEARCH Q1.
