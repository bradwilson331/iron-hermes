<objective>
Land the full operator-facing CLI verb surface (D-33: ~20 verbs + 4 operator-recovery verbs) and the slash-command propagation (D-35). This is the surface humans + scripts + cron use to drive the board; the LLM tools from plan 04 are the parallel surface for workers. Both must agree by construction — they both route through `KanbanStore` directly.

Purpose: Without a CLI, the board cannot be inspected or driven manually; tasks created by orchestrator profiles via `kanban_create` would have no human-readable inspection path. The `CommandRouter` (Phase 21.1) propagates the new `kanban` `CommandDef` entry to TG/Discord/Slack automatically by construction — adding one registry row gives you `/kanban` across every gateway platform. D-36 requires `is_bypass()` to accept `kanban` so the slash command works mid-turn (the board lives in DB, not in the running agent's context).

Output: A `kanban` subcommand on the `Commands` enum, a `kanban/` module owning all per-verb implementations, the registry entry, and the bypass-list extension with its test mirror.
</objective>
