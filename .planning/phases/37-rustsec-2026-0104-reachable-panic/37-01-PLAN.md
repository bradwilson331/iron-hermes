<objective>
Remediate RUSTSEC-2026-0104 — a reachable panic (DoS, CVSS 7.5) in `rustls-webpki`
CRL parsing — for the patchable dependency chain, and formally document the
non-patchable chain as an accepted, tracked exemption.

Purpose: Close the actively-exploitable Chain 2 (rustls 0.23.x consumers:
reqwest, hyper-rustls, slack-morphism, chromiumoxide) by forcing
`rustls-webpki` to the fixed `0.103.13`. Chain 1 (serenity → tokio-tungstenite
0.21 → rustls 0.22.4 → rustls-webpki 0.102.8) has NO upstream patch (serenity
0.12.5 is latest; no 0.13.x exists) and is semver-incompatible with the 0.103.x
patch, so it is risk-accepted and documented, not fixed.

Output: A `[patch.crates-io]` block in root `Cargo.toml` with an exact pin and an
explanatory exemption comment; a regenerated `Cargo.lock`; a workspace build +
full test suite confirming no regressions.
</objective>
