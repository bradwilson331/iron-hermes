## Known Stubs

**Transitional worker body in runner.rs (lines ~960-1050):**
The worker spawned on `DispatchOutcome::WorkerSpawned` currently exits immediately when the queue drains (no Notify-based idle wait). This means a burst of messages to the same chat may spawn multiple workers in sequence rather than a single persistent worker. This is intentional — Plan 02's exclusive scope is to rewrite this worker body with the full Notify-based pop loop (D-04, D-05, D-06). The transitional worker is correct for single-message dispatch and does not lose messages (SessionQueue holds them); only the multi-message-per-worker efficiency is missing until Plan 02.
